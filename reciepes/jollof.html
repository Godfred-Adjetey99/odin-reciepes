Jollof

Jollof rice is a popular seasoned rice dish originating from West Africa, specifically the Senegambian region (modern-day Senegal and The Gambia), where it likely stems from the Wolof Empire. It is typically prepared by simmering long-grain rice in a rich, spiced tomato-based stew containing peppers, onions, and various herbs.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • paprika

Instructions

  1. 1 cup of long-grain rice
  2. 2 tablespoons of oil
  3. 1 large onion, chopped
  4. 3 cloves of garlic, minced
  5. 1 teaspoon of ground ginger
  6. 1 teaspoon of cumin
  7. 1 teaspoon of paprika
  8. 1 can of diced tomatoes
  9. 1 cup of chicken broth
  10. Salt and pepper to taste
