Lasagna

Lasagna, also known by the plural lasagne, is a type of pasta made in wide, flat sheets that originates from Italian cuisine. It is best known as a baked dish (pasta al forno) featuring stacked layers of pasta alternating with fillings such as ragù (meat and tomato sauce), béchamel, ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Tomato paste
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Parsley
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large skillet, cook ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  3. Add chopped onions and garlic to the skillet and sauté until softened.
  4. Stir in tomato sauce, tomato paste, and seasonings. Simmer for 15-20 minutes.
  5. In a separate bowl, mix ricotta cheese with egg, parsley, and a pinch of salt.
  6. In a baking dish, spread a layer of meat sauce, followed by a layer of lasagna noodles, then a layer of the ricotta mixture. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with meat sauce on top.
  7. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella and Parmesan cheese over the top.
  8. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes or until cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  9. Let the lasagna rest for 10-15 minutes before serving.
